About Me

I've been that
international student.

I'm Haoxuan (Daisy) Tang, a first-year graduate student in Computer Software Engineering at Northeastern University's Seattle campus. I grew up in China, completed my graduate studies in the UK, and am now navigating a new city and a new career path in the US — which means I've experienced firsthand what it means to arrive somewhere unfamiliar and figure it out.

At the University of Nottingham, I served for nearly a year as an International Student Ambassador in the Faculty of Engineering — a formal, paid role where I ran bilingual Q&A sessions, built community resources for incoming students from around the world, organized intercultural events, and helped hundreds of prospective students navigate both the academic and cultural transition to life in the UK.

That lived experience — and the empathy that comes with it — is what I bring to the GLS Ambassador role.
